# Settings

| Setting | Default | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ignored apps | none | Added from the running apps (menu bar apps too) or any app chosen with Other…; nothing copied in them is recorded |

The history size (10) is fixed.

- Paste from Other Apps off (macOS enforces pasteboard privacy and Vortex isn't allowed): a row "⚠ Paste from Other Apps · Keeps your clipboard history." with "Turn on", which opens the shell's page; ↻ shows "Works after restart" with "Restart Vortex"; `notNeeded` (macOS 26.4) shows no row.
- The section re-reads the statuses when it appears (`PermissionsClient.refresh`).
